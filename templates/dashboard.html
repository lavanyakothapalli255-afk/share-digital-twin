What-If Twin Simulation

This module applies controlled mathematical perturbations to the observed physiological state and recomputes the Digital Twin's baseline-relative state. It demonstrates how the Twin representation responds to hypothetical physiological changes.

⚠ Important limitation The SHARE dataset does not contain intervention-response data. Therefore these simulations are counterfactual engineering demonstrations and should not be interpreted as treatment effects, clinical recommendations, or validated individual risk estimates.
